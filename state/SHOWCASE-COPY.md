# Showcase copy

## 1. What it is, who it is for, why it is useful  (941/1000 chars, small caps)

> FALSIFIABLE IS A STAKE MARKET ON CLAIMS ABOUT A PUBLIC WEB PAGE. ONE HTTPS URL, ONE PRECISELY-WORDED CLAIM, ONE MACHINE-CHECKABLE RULE, ONE CLOSE TIME. PEOPLE BACK THE CLAIM WITH GEN; WHEN THE WINDOW CLOSES, VALIDATORS FETCH THE PAGE THEMSELVES AND RULE YES, NO OR UNDETERMINED. THE WINNING SIDE SPLITS THE WHOLE POT PRO RATA; UNDETERMINED AND ONE-SIDED POOLS REFUND EVERYONE. IT IS FOR PEOPLE WHO WANT TO BACK SOMETHING THEY CAN CHECK, AND FOR PEOPLE WHO DO NOT TRUST A MODEL'S OPINION OF A PAGE. WHAT MAKES IT USEFUL: THE ANSWER IS BYTES AGAINST A PUBLISHED RULE, NOT A MODEL, SO NOTHING A PAGE SAYS CAN CHANGE THE OUTCOME; EVERY VALIDATOR RECOMPUTES THE SHA-256 OF THE EXACT BYTES IT READ, AND THE HASH IS ON CHAIN; SHARES SUM TO THE POT EXACTLY, WITH NO ROUNDING DUST; THERE ARE NO FEES, NO APPEALS, NO ADMIN AND NO OWNER; AND A MARKET NOBODY RULES ON REFUNDS ITSELF, SO FUNDS CANNOT LOCK. THE FRONT END READS THE CHAIN AND HOLDS NO KEY.

## 2. What the visitor should do  (228/400 chars)

> OPEN THE BOARD AND PICK A MARKET. READ THE RULE, NOT THE PROSE. COPY THE STAKE COMMAND, RUN IT WITH YOUR OWN KEY, AND WAIT FOR THE VALIDATORS. WHEN IT RESOLVES, COPY THE WITHDRAW COMMAND AND RUN IT. THE SITE NEVER TOUCHES A KEY.

## 3. What the steward should see afterwards  (411/500 chars)

> THE STEWARD SEES A NEW CONTRACT ADDRESS, A MARKET ID, THE CLAIM AND THE RULE AS PUBLISHED, AND A CLOSE TIME. THE DEPLOYED SOURCE IS PROVEN BY SHA-256 TO BE BYTE-IDENTICAL TO THE REPOSITORY FILE. AFTER THE CLOSE, THE RECORD SHOWS THE OUTCOME, THE SHA-256 OF THE EXACT PAGE BYTES THE VALIDATORS READ, THE POT, AND EVERY WINNER'S PAYOUT - PAYABLE ONLY TO ITS OWN ADDRESS. THE TRANSACTION IDS ARE IN THE REPOSITORY.
